# Credits

Pokéwild is an unofficial fan game. Pokémon and Pokémon character names and artwork belong to Nintendo, Creatures Inc., and GAME FREAK. This game is not affiliated with or endorsed by those companies.

Species names, generation information, base stats, capture rates, legendary and mythical classifications, type matchups, and evolution paths were bundled from the [PokéAPI dataset](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/tree/master/data/v2/csv), accessed September 12, 2026. Special evolution conditions are adapted to Pokéwild’s level goals; they do not reproduce every requirement from the original games.

Pokémon sprites, all 1,025 shiny species sprites, and selected official artwork were bundled from the [PokéAPI sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). Their presence in the repository does not transfer ownership of the Pokémon artwork.

Mega Evolution and Gigantamax eligibility, form types, and base stats were retrieved from the public [PokéAPI Pokémon endpoint](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon?limit=2000) on September 27, 2026. All 131 form choices include normal and shiny images from the same PokéAPI sprites repository, resized for this game. Transformation duration, access requirements, move power, and alternate-style selection are adapted to Pokéwild’s simplified battle rules.

The landscape illustrations, interface icons, game implementation, and visual layout were created for this game. Badge illustrations were redrawn for this interface. Gym and trainer lineups and rewards are adapted for Pokéwild’s difficulty and do not reproduce a particular official game.

Clover Town, the three explorable routes, quests, trainer avatar illustrations, care activities, move progression, and tournament structure were created for this fan game. Friendship, shiny encounter rates, and move availability are simplified game rules.

Fonts are [Outfit](https://github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts) and [DM Sans](https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts), distributed under the SIL Open Font License. License texts are included in `assets/licenses/`.
